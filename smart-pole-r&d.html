Beyond
A Light Pole.

The Smart Pole is envisioned as more than a conventional lighting structure.

It provides an engineered platform where lighting, sensors, communication technologies, monitoring and connected infrastructure can converge.

The modular approach allows the platform to evolve according to application, environment and technology requirements.

From Physical Infrastructure To Digital Data.

The Smart Pole can act as an edge point between physical infrastructure and digital systems.

Lighting status, equipment information and connected sensor data can be structured for centralized monitoring and analysis.

This creates a foundation for future smart-city applications without requiring the entire infrastructure to be redesigned.
