Testing Discipline

Energization is a process. Not a switch.

The final testing plan is project and equipment specific. The objective is to verify insulation, wiring, protection, interlocks, earthing, control logic and switching readiness before the system is energized.

02VCB/RMU functional and interlock checks
03Protection relay and trip-circuit verification

05Transformer pre-commissioning checks
