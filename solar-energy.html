01 / ON-GRID SOLAR
Grid Connected Solar

Use solar first. Use the grid when needed.

On-Grid solar works together with the utility grid. Solar generation serves the connected loads during the day, while grid power supports demand whenever solar generation is insufficient. Where regulations permit, surplus energy can be exported through the applicable metering arrangement.

01Designed for electricity-bill reduction and daytime load offset
02No mandatory battery bank, keeping the architecture simpler
03Suitable for homes, offices, institutions and industrial rooftops
04Can include remote generation and inverter monitoring
Best for: Sites with reliable grid availability and a strong objective to reduce electricity cost through daytime solar generation.
02 / OFF-GRID SOLAR
Independent Solar Power

Generate. Store. Use power when the grid cannot.

Off-Grid solar combines solar generation with battery storage so the site can operate independently of the utility network. System sizing must consider load profile, autonomy hours, battery capacity and seasonal solar availability.

01Battery storage for night-time use and backup continuity
02Useful for remote locations and unreliable-grid environments
03Engineered around essential loads and required backup duration
04Can support hybrid power architectures with additional sources
Best for: Remote sites, farms, facilities with frequent outages, critical loads and locations where dependable backup is the primary requirement.
03 / GROUND-MOUNTED SOLAR
Large-Scale Solar Plants

When the project needs space, scale and serious output.

Ground-Mounted solar uses available land to build higher-capacity PV plants with engineered row spacing, tilt, access roads, evacuation planning, protection, monitoring and long-term maintainability in mind.

01Suitable for industrial captive use, campuses and MW-scale plants
02Layout optimized for land geometry, shading and energy yield
03Electrical evacuation, protection and SCADA options as required
04Designed for service access, cleaning and long-term O&M
Best for: Industries, large institutions, developers and infrastructure projects with suitable land and higher energy demand.
