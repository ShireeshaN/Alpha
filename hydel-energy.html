Hydel Technology

Every stage matters.
Every flow creates power.

Efficient hydel energy generation depends on coordinated water management, hydraulic systems, turbines, generators, electrical infrastructure and intelligent control.

01

Water Resource

Flowing or falling water provides the hydraulic resource required for renewable hydel power generation.

02

Hydraulic System

Water intake, channels, penstocks and associated hydraulic infrastructure guide water toward the turbine system.

03

Turbine Conversion

Hydraulic energy is converted into mechanical rotational energy through appropriately selected turbine systems.

04

Generator & Protection

Generators, control systems and protection equipment convert turbine power into electrical energy while supporting safe operation.

05

Energy Monitoring

Monitor water flow, turbine performance, generator output, energy flow, equipment status and operational performance through connected systems.
