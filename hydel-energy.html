Hydropower Generation Process

Water To Electricity

A coordinated sequence converts the energy of water into reliable electrical power.

01

Water Storage & Intake

Water is stored or diverted and directed toward the hydroelectric system through controlled intake structures.

02

Water Conduction

Water flows through canals, tunnels, penstocks or other conductor systems toward the turbine.

03

Turbine Rotation

Hydraulic energy rotates the turbine runner and produces mechanical energy.

04

Electrical Generation

The turbine drives the generator to produce electrical power.

05

Voltage Transformation

Generated electricity is stepped up through transformers for efficient power transfer.

06

Protection & Control

Control, monitoring and protection systems support safe and stable operation.

07

Switchyard

Electrical power is routed through the switchyard and associated equipment.

08

Grid Integration

Generated electricity is evacuated and integrated into the transmission network.
