Wind Turbine Systems

Integrated Wind Generation Architecture

A wind turbine combines mechanical and electrical systems to convert wind energy into usable electrical power.

01

Rotor Blades

Aerodynamic blades capture kinetic energy from moving wind.

02

Hub & Rotor

Transfers rotational movement from blades into the turbine drivetrain.

03

Nacelle

Houses key mechanical and electrical turbine equipment.

04

Gearbox / Drive System

Transfers and manages rotational energy for power generation.

05

Generator

Converts mechanical rotational energy into electrical energy.

06

Tower Structure

Supports the turbine at an elevated position for effective wind capture.

07

Transformer

Steps up generated voltage for electrical collection and transmission.

08

Control & Monitoring

Monitors turbine parameters and supports safe and efficient operation.
