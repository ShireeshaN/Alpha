IoT, not BMS

A digital nervous system for connected operations.

Our IoT scope starts at the device and data layer. We connect sensors, controllers, meters, machines and existing equipment, normalize their data, move intelligence to the edge or cloud, and give teams one reliable operational view.

BMS remains a separate building-management discipline. IoT is the wider connectivity and data architecture that can serve factories, utilities, infrastructure, energy systems, campuses, logistics, agriculture, equipment fleets and connected products.
