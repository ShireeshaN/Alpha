How It Works

From Sensor to Action

Intelligent home automation combines sensors, controllers and connected devices to respond automatically to changing conditions.

01

Sense

Sensors detect motion, temperature, occupancy and environmental changes.

02

Connect

Devices communicate through the automation network.

03

Process

The automation controller evaluates configured rules and scenes.

04

Act

Connected devices automatically respond to the required action.

05

Control

Users can monitor and control systems through apps, panels or voice.
