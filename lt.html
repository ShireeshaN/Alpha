Testing Discipline

Installed is not enough. Verified matters.

Testing scope is finalized against equipment type, approved procedures, OEM instructions and project requirements. The objective is simple: confirm that the installed system is safe, correctly connected and ready for controlled energization.

01Insulation resistance and continuity verification
02Earth resistance and earth-continuity checks
03Breaker, interlock and control-circuit functional testing
04Protection relay / metering checks as applicable
05Cable and termination inspection before energization
06Pre-commissioning records and handover documentation
